# Skunkflix Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 8 August 2026**

This privacy policy explains, in plain language, what Skunkflix uses, where information goes, and what choices you have.

## 1. What Skunkflix is

Skunkflix is an independent media-player app that connects to Plex accounts and Plex Media Servers chosen by the user. Skunkflix does not run the Plex service and does not provide, host, sell, or distribute a media library of its own.

## 2. Information the app uses

To make the app work, Skunkflix may use:

- Your Plex user identifier.
- Your Plex authentication token.
- Details about the Plex Media Server you choose.
- A device/client identifier used to identify the app to Plex.
- Search requests you make in the app.
- Media selections, playback activity, watch progress, and watchlist actions.
- App preferences stored on your device.

This information is used only to provide app functions such as signing in, finding servers you are allowed to access, loading your media library, searching, playing media, saving watch progress, managing your watchlist, and remembering app settings.

## 3. Where information is stored and sent

Plex authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. App preferences, selected-server information, and a client identifier are stored locally on your device.

Skunkflix does not operate its own user-account database or analytics backend. Information needed for Plex features is sent directly to Plex services or to the Plex Media Server you choose.

## 4. Offline downloads

If you choose to download eligible media, the file is downloaded from a Plex Media Server you are authorized to access and stored in Skunkflix's private app storage on your device for offline playback. Skunkflix does not upload that media to a Skunkflix server and does not share it with other users.

## 5. Chromecast and Google Cast

Skunkflix includes the Google Cast SDK so you can discover and control Cast-enabled devices. Google may process device identifiers, product interaction information, and diagnostic information for Cast functionality and analytics under Google's own privacy terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Advertising, selling data, and tracking

Skunkflix does not contain advertising. Skunkflix does not sell your personal information. Skunkflix does not use your information for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Skunkflix keeps locally stored settings and credentials only while they are needed for the app to work or until you remove them.

You can:

- Use **Sign Out and Forget Plex** in Skunkflix to remove saved Plex credentials from the device.
- Delete offline media from the Downloads screen.
- Delete the Skunkflix app to remove its locally stored app data from the device, subject to normal iOS backup and restore behaviour.
- Revoke Local Network access in iOS Settings.

Skunkflix cannot directly delete information held by Plex or Google. Their own retention and deletion rules apply to information they process. You can manage your Plex account through Plex and your Google information through Google.

## 8. Your choices and consent

You can choose not to connect a Plex account, revoke permissions, remove downloads, sign out, or delete the app. Some Skunkflix features will not work when the connection or permission required for that feature is disabled.

## 9. Third-party services

Skunkflix works with third-party services, including Plex and Google Cast. Those services are separate from Skunkflix and have their own privacy policies and terms. Skunkflix only shares information with third-party services when needed for app functionality and requires integrated third-party services to protect user information in accordance with their published privacy terms and applicable privacy requirements.

Plex Privacy & Legal: https://www.plex.tv/about/privacy-legal/

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 10. Children

Skunkflix is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to Skunkflix. Parents and guardians should manage access to Plex accounts and media libraries appropriately.

## 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Skunkflix features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version of the policy takes effect.
